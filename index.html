Know Your World

The Seven Kingdoms of Westeros span a continent shaped by conquest, betrayal, and fire. Three hundred years before Robert's Rebellion, Aegon the Conqueror and his sister-wives Visenya and Rhaenys united the kingdoms beneath the Iron Throne — a seat forged from the swords of defeated enemies, bound together by the breath of Balerion the Black Dread.

The great houses — Stark of Winterfell, Lannister of Casterly Rock, Baratheon of Storm's End, Targaryen of Dragonstone — each carry words, sigils, and centuries of blood. Beyond the Wall, the Night's Watch holds a crumbling vigil. Across the Narrow Sea, the Free Cities and the slaver ports of Essos play their own game.

Five questions stand between you and glory. Answer wisely. The realm is watching.
